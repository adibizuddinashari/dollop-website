This Cookie Policy explains how Beyond Bites Sdn Bhd, trading as Dollop ("Dollop", "we", "us", or "our"), uses cookies and similar technologies on dollopgelato.com (the "Website"). It should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit. They are widely used to make websites work, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to the site owner. "Similar technologies" include pixels, web beacons, software development kits, and browser storage such as localStorage, which can be used for comparable purposes.

Cookies set by the website you are visiting are called first-party cookies. Cookies set by another domain are called third-party cookies; these enable third-party features or functionality, such as analytics, maps, or advertising.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • keep the Website working and secure, and remember what is in your order;
  • remember basic preferences and site configuration between visits;
  • understand how visitors use the Website so we can improve it; and
  • where enabled, measure the performance of our marketing and show relevant offers.

We do not use cookies to collect sensitive personal information, and we do not sell the information collected through cookies.

3. Types of cookies we use

Strictly necessary

These are required for the Website to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually set only in response to actions you take, such as adding items to your order or navigating pages. The Website does not currently set first-party tracking cookies of its own for this purpose; essential state is held in your browser's local storage (see section 5).

Functional

These help the Website remember choices you make and provide enhanced features. We use browser local storage to keep your shopping cart (dollop_cart) and a cached copy of site configuration and announcements (dollop_config) so pages load correctly and your order is not lost as you browse.

Analytics & performance

Where enabled, these let us count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the Website's performance — for example through Google Analytics. They tell us which pages are most and least popular and how visitors move around the site. If we are not currently running an analytics tool, no analytics cookies are set.

Advertising

Where enabled, these are set through our Website by advertising partners such as Meta (Facebook) Pixel and Google. They may be used to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant ads on other sites. Our checkout is built to send standard events (such as "InitiateCheckout" and "Purchase") to Meta Pixel only if a pixel is active on the Website. If no advertising pixel is installed, no advertising cookies are set.

4. Third-party cookies & services

Some cookies and requests come from third parties whose services we use to run the Website:

  • Google Fonts — serves the fonts used on the Website.
  • Google Maps — the map showing our location; Google may set cookies and use your approximate location when the map loads.
  • Google (Apps Script / Forms) — receives and processes your order and delivers site configuration.
  • Lenis (served via a CDN) — provides smooth scrolling; it does not set cookies.
  • Meta (Facebook) Pixel / Google Ads — advertising and measurement, where enabled.
  • WhatsApp — used off-site to confirm orders and take payment.

These third parties are responsible for their own cookies and processing. Please review their privacy and cookie notices for details and for their opt-out options.

5. Local storage

In addition to cookies, the Website uses your browser's localStorage. Unlike cookies, this data is not sent to our servers with each request — it stays in your browser and is used to keep your cart and cached settings. You can clear it at any time through your browser's "clear site data" or "clear browsing data" settings; doing so will empty your cart.

6. How to manage cookies

Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies. The methods vary between browsers and versions, but you can usually manage cookies through the browser's settings or preferences menu. Blocking or deleting cookies may affect how the Website works — for example, your order may not be remembered.

  • Guidance for common browsers is available from their help pages (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge).
  • To opt out of Google Analytics across all websites, install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • To manage interest-based advertising, see optout.networkadvertising.org and youronlinechoices.com, and your ad settings with Google and Meta.

Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before setting non-essential cookies and will give you a way to withdraw it.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the technologies we use or for legal reasons. The updated version is indicated by the "Last updated" date above and takes effect when published. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
